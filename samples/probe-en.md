# The London Account — a probe for the English reading version

This text is a probe. It reads like a chapter, but it is built so that
every rule in the English reading rules appears in it at least once, and
in the place where it would appear in real prose. Spoken without being
prepared first, it produces every mistake a speech model can make.

## Quantities that are not identifiers

books is not the billion of the newer ones, and the text has to say which.
